# lr-portal notes

Feature rules for lr-portal, split out of `CLAUDE.md` (which keeps the cross-cutting rules: stack, conventions, styling, auth, forms, tables, data cache…). Open this folder in Obsidian as a vault; the graph view follows the `[[links]]`.

- [[sales-recording|Sales recording]] — Recording project / rental / brokerage sales, commission slips & receiving, cutoffs, international sales
- [[sales-validation|Sales validation]] — Validating sales by area / category, status changes, sale review drawer
- [[registration-onboarding|Registration, onboarding & NAO]] — Become a Salesperson / Global Partner sign-up, /welcome steps, NAO + NAO approvals, the journey page
- [[fire|FIRE]] — realestatetraining.ph integration: modules, exams, Rent.ph module 12, endorsement letter, certificates
- [[sms|SMS Manager]] — Semaphore SMS: recipients picker, credits & requests, send job, Management section
- [[profile|Profile, business profile & preferences]] — /profile, avatars, business profile & contact page, Settings › Preferences
- [[dashboard|Dashboard]] — Shared dashboard, My Sales, Overview, partner & dashboard posters, LR ID
- [[reports|Reports & statistics]] — Sales report / Reports cards, report layout, areas (branches), Recruits, Recruits Progress, statistics charts, leaderboards
- [[natcon|NATCON]] — NATCON boards, periods / cutoffs, reach, My NATCON sales
- [[management|Management]] — Management cards / tabs, agents & teams, transfers, designations, editing profile & upline, agent activity, documentation
- [[people-and-reach|People & reach]] — Global Partners, Genealogy, office secretaries, My Team — who sees whom
- [[superadmin-tools|Superadmin tools]] — Sign in as a user (impersonation), View as role
- [[legacy-data|Legacy data]] — Imports from lr_legacy: members, sales, teams, ids, registration status, fill-existing
- [[search|Global search]] — Navbar search: pages, people, groups; sanctioned JSON endpoints

Architecture survey: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. Legacy mappings: `docs/legacy-migration/`.
